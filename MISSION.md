# Mission: Practical AI fluency

## Why

Help hesitant and experienced workplace learners enter a positive learning loop: complete one useful, safe, independently verified task, then turn that success into better judgment or a repeatable workflow.

## Success looks like

- Learners produce a verified artifact instead of only watching demonstrations.
- Learners explain the shared vocabulary of agentic work in plain language.
- Learners choose either Building with AI 201 or AI Thinking 201 without a hidden cross-track prerequisite.
- Learners preserve human decision ownership and require evidence before use.
- Learners complete a second verified task within seven days.

## Constraints

- No more than 20 lessons across Foundations and both 201 courses; this release contains 20, leaving no headroom.
- Each lesson teaches one concept in 1–3 minutes and ends with an observable action.
- Product-specific claims must be checked against official documentation and the approved environment.
- Examples use approved, non-sensitive material and begin read-only.

## Out of scope

- Building with AI 301 and AI Thinking 301 content.
- Production agent orchestration, custom MCP implementation, and systematic evaluation engineering.
- Inventing organization policy, access, models, connectors, or release decisions.
