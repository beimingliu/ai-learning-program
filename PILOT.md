# Pilot Protocol

## Cohort

Recruit at least six learners:

- One Docs or Excel user.
- One Tableau or SQL analyst.
- One Python or early agentic-coding user.
- One manager or lead.
- At least two people who use AI rarely or hesitantly.

## Run

1. Observe Foundations 101 without intervening except for access or policy blockers.
2. Require the Foundation evidence card before offering a 201 route.
3. Assign learners to the 201 track they choose; do not require the other track.
4. Collect each track's capstone.
5. Follow up after seven and thirty days.

## Behavioral measures

- Access rate: reached a working approved prompt.
- First-win rate: produced one useful, verified artifact.
- Seven-day second-win rate: completed another verified task.
- Thirty-day repeat rate: reran a saved workflow.
- Verified-use rate: documented at least one source reconciliation.
- Track continuation rate: began either 201 course.
- Capstone completion rate: produced the required course artifact.

Do not use prompt count, token use, or time in the tool as success measures.

## Interview questions

- When did the tool begin to feel useful rather than intimidating?
- Which term remained unclear?
- Where did you pause or leave the course?
- Which check made you comfortable using the result?
- Could you name a second task to try within seven days?
- Did your chosen 201 track depend on material from the other track?
- What should be removed, reordered, or demonstrated differently?

## Revision gate

Revise a lesson when multiple learners miss its action, misunderstand its boundary, or need the other 201 track to complete it. Record changes in the product-accuracy log and rerun the affected task with at least one hesitant learner.
