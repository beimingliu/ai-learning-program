# AI Learning Program

This is the complete first release of the shared Foundations course and two parallel 201 courses described in the August 2026 handoff.

## Start here

1. Complete [AI Foundations 101](courses/foundations-101/syllabus.md).
2. Continue with either [Building with AI 201](courses/building-with-ai/201/syllabus.md) or [AI Thinking 201](courses/ai-thinking/201/syllabus.md).
3. The two 201 courses are independent. Neither is a prerequisite for the other.

Open [course-progress-mind-map.html](course-progress-mind-map.html) for the standalone progress mind map, or [learner-course.html](learner-course.html) for the lesson-card view. Facilitators and course producers should also read the [facilitator guide](shared/facilitator-guide.md).

## Course inventory

| Course | Lessons | Approximate lesson time | Required artifact |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| AI Foundations 101 | 8 | 31 minutes | Verified artifact + evidence card |

Capstones, placement exercises, and course introductions are not counted as lessons.

## What is complete

- Course briefs, prerequisites, outcomes, lesson maps, capstones, and assessment rubrics.
- Twenty fully scripted lessons with on-screen cues, exercises, boundaries, sources, and next-level bridges.
- Learner workbook, facilitator guidance, source map, product-accuracy log, and pilot protocol.
- Canonical [wording and content reference](shared/wording-content-reference.md) for future script tone revisions.
- Interactive learner course map with local progress tracking.
- Structural and content checks recorded in the [QA report](shared/qa-report.md).
