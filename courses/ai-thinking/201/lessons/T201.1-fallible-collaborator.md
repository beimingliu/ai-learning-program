# T201.1 — AI Is a Fallible Collaborator, Not an Oracle

**Duration:** 2–3 minutes  
**Audience assumption:** The learner completed or placed out of Foundations 101  
**Key concept:** Use AI for leverage while keeping evidence and consequential decisions outside its confidence.  
**Learner outcome:** Calibrate one real AI use across leverage, evidence, and human ownership.

## A-roll script

Welcome to the Analytics AI Enablement education series. Today in AI Thinking, we focus on AI as a fallible collaborator.

Two reactions make AI hard to use well. “It makes mistakes, so it is useless” throws away leverage. “It sounds confident, so it must be right” throws away judgment.

Treat AI as a **fallible collaborator**. A collaborator can inspect more material, draft alternatives, transform formats, or surface patterns quickly. Fallible means the output can be incomplete, subtly wrong, or persuasive beyond the evidence.

Calibrate each task with three questions.

First, where is the **leverage**? Name the concrete work AI makes easier: retrieving, comparing, drafting, checking, or exploring.

Second, what **evidence** would make the contribution usable? That may be a source passage, a recomputed total, a test, a known example, or review by a domain expert.

Third, who owns the **decision**? AI may recommend a staffing change, identify a possible risk, or draft a customer response. A person still owns the judgment and any consequential action.

Consider a variance summary. AI can compare exports and draft an explanation. Exact totals and source rows provide evidence. The analyst decides whether the variance is real and what the business should do. Neither reflexive distrust nor blind trust fits that arrangement.

The boundary is that human review is not automatically reliable either. A rushed reviewer can rubber-stamp fluent output. The human role must include a real check and a clear decision responsibility.

Choose one current task. Write one line for leverage, one for evidence, and one for the human decision. If any line is blank, the collaboration is not yet designed.

## Immediate exercise and feedback loop

## Boundary

Human involvement without an explicit check or decision is not meaningful oversight.
