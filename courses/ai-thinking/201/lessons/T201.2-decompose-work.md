# T201.2 — Decompose the Work and Preserve Human Judgment

**Duration:** 2–3 minutes  
**Audience assumption:** The learner can separate AI leverage from human decision ownership  
**Key concept:** Assign AI and human roles at the level of work steps, not whole jobs.  
**Learner outcome:** Decompose one task and mark where a human must decide.

## A-roll script

Welcome to the Analytics AI Enablement education series. Today in AI Thinking, we focus on decomposing work and preserving human judgment.

“Can AI do this job?” is usually too large a question. A job contains different kinds of work, with different evidence and accountability.

Break one task into steps. Common steps include **inspect**, **retrieve**, **draft**, **transform**, **recommend**, **decide**, **execute**, and **verify**. Then assign a role to each step.

AI is often useful for inspecting many sources, retrieving candidates, drafting a first version, transforming formats, or generating alternatives. A human should own decisions that depend on accountability, values, sensitive context, or consequences the model cannot bear. Execution may be delegated only when access, permissions, reversibility, and governance support it. Verification should be independent of the same unsupported claim.

Take a monthly performance review. AI might inspect approved metrics, draft a variance table, and suggest questions. The manager checks the numbers, brings relationship and organizational context, decides the evaluation, and conducts the conversation. “AI writes the review” hides these distinctions and invites accidental delegation of judgment.

Now add boundaries. What must the AI not infer? Which sources are authoritative? What action is irreversible? Who is accountable if the recommendation is wrong? Where must the workflow stop for a human?

The boundary is that decomposition should clarify ownership, not create a maze of tiny handoffs. Combine adjacent low-risk steps when the same inputs, checks, and owner apply.

Choose one task from your week. List five to eight steps. Label the AI role and human role for each, then circle the consequential decision and write the responsible person's name or role beside it.

## Boundary

Decomposition is useful only when it clarifies work and ownership; excessive handoffs add coordination cost.
