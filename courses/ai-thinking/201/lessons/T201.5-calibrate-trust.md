# T201.5 — Calibrate Trust With Evidence and Uncertainty

**Duration:** 2–3 minutes  
**Audience assumption:** The learner can diagnose failures across the agentic system  
**Key concept:** The evidence burden rises with consequence, uncertainty, and irreversibility.  
**Learner outcome:** Set an evidence level and escalation rule appropriate to one task's risk.

## A-roll script

Welcome to the Analytics AI Enablement education series. Today in AI Thinking, we focus on calibrating trust.

Fluent language creates a feeling of certainty. Trust should come from evidence, not presentation.

Use an evidence ladder. At the bottom is an unsupported answer: useful for brainstorming, not for factual use. Next is a traceable answer with named sources, citations, calculations, or tool output. Stronger is an **independent check**: you reconcile a total, run a test, inspect a known record, or compare with an authoritative system. Stronger still is **multiple evidence**: several representative checks, an evaluation set, or review by a qualified domain owner.

Choose the rung by risk. A reversible draft for internal brainstorming may need clear labeling and a quick source check. A recurring operational report needs coverage checks, trusted totals, and known examples. A recommendation affecting money, employment, health, legal rights, safety, or external commitments needs stronger evidence, documented uncertainty, and the accountable domain owner. Some tasks should not proceed with AI at all under policy.

Separate three statements: what the evidence supports, what remains uncertain, and what decision a human must make. Ask the agent to label assumptions and contradictory evidence, but verify those labels yourself; self-reported uncertainty is still part of the output.

One check can fail by coincidence or poor coverage. If the task is consequential, sample across cases or use a formal review process rather than repeating the easiest example.

The boundary is that no evidence removes all uncertainty. The objective is not absolute certainty; it is a decision process whose evidence and ownership are proportional to impact.

Choose one task, name its consequence if wrong, select the evidence rung, and write the condition that requires escalation or stopping.

## Boundary

Evidence reduces uncertainty; it does not eliminate it or transfer accountability away from the human decision owner.
