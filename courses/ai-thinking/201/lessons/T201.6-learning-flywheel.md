# T201.6 — Build a Personal and Team Learning Flywheel

**Duration:** 2–3 minutes  
**Audience assumption:** The learner can design work with ownership, evidence, and stop conditions  
**Key concept:** A verified use becomes capability when its task, evidence, failure, and reusable pattern improve the next run.  
**Learner outcome:** Schedule a second verified task and share one decision-grade learning.

## A-roll script

Welcome to the Analytics AI Enablement education series. Today in AI Thinking, we focus on building a learning flywheel.

A completed course is not the adoption milestone. The second verified task begins the habit.

Use a simple flywheel: **task, evidence, pattern, share, repeat**. Choose a contained real task. Record the source, model and tool path if relevant, time or quality effect, checks performed, and what remained a human decision. Then extract the reusable part: a task brief, checklist, workflow, example, or warning.

Share verified failures as well as successes. “The summary was wrong because the current-quarter file was never loaded; the coverage check caught it” teaches more than “AI saved me time.” Psychological safety matters: people will hide weak attempts if the team celebrates only smooth demos.

Managers can reinforce the flywheel with better questions. Ask: What outcome became easier? Which evidence made the result usable? What failed, and what changed? What should another person reuse? Do not rank people by prompt count, token use, or time in the tool; activity can represent leverage or confusion.

Give reusable assets an owner and review date. Business definitions, tools, policies, and model behavior change. A stale example can teach the wrong lesson with the confidence of documentation.

The boundary is that sharing does not permit exposing sensitive prompts, data, credentials, or outputs. Remove private details while preserving the decision-grade pattern and evidence.

Before leaving, choose the task and date for a second verified use within seven days. Write the two-sentence note you will share: what you tried, and which context or check made the result usable—or showed that it was not.

## Immediate exercise and feedback loop

Schedule the second task and draft the two-sentence learning note. At seven days, update it with actual evidence and one reusable asset.

## Boundary

Share patterns and evidence only within data policy; remove sensitive content and assign maintenance ownership.
