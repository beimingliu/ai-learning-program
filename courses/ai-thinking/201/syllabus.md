# AI Thinking 201 — Working and Reasoning With AI

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last content review:** August 12, 2026  
**Prerequisite:** AI Foundations 101 or its placement exercise  
**Length:** Six lessons, approximately 17 minutes, plus a 30–45 minute capstone

## Lesson format

Every lesson opens by naming the Analytics AI Enablement education series, the AI Thinking track, and the lesson topic. It closes by inviting learners to explore other videos and send feedback.

Each series bookend must take no more than 10 seconds of narration.

## Promise

Frame work well, preserve human judgment, reason in feedback loops, diagnose failure, and require evidence proportional to risk—regardless of the AI interface used.

## Audience

Analysts, managers, domain experts, and technical users who need better judgment and collaboration patterns more than product configuration.

## Outcomes

By the end, the learner can treat AI as a fallible collaborator, decompose work into AI and human responsibilities, iterate with progress checks, diagnose failures across the system, calibrate trust with evidence and uncertainty, and turn verified learning into a personal or team flywheel.

## Capstone: AI Work Design Canvas

Redesign one real task using the AI Work Design Canvas in the [learner workbook](../../../shared/learner-workbook.md). The task may use any organization-approved interface; no Building with AI 201 mechanics are required.

### Rubric

Score each criterion 0, 1, or 2. Passing is 10/12 with no zero on decision ownership or evidence.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome and scope | Activity only | Partial outcome | Observable outcome, constraints, and non-goals |
| Work allocation | “AI does it” | Some division | Specific AI roles and human responsibilities by step |
| Decision ownership | Unassigned | Reviewer implied | Named human owner for consequential judgment/action |
| Evidence | Trust by fluency | One generic check | Evidence type and strength match the task risk |
| Failure handling | Retry indefinitely | Generic escalation | Diagnostic, stop, and escalation conditions are explicit |
| Reuse and learning | One-off use | Notes retained | Reusable asset, owner, and next learning loop defined |

## Independence rule

Building with AI 201 is optional enrichment. The capstone can be completed on paper or with any approved interface and does not require product configuration.
