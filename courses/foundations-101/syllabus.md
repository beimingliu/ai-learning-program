# AI Foundations 101 — The Common Core

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last content review:** August 20, 2026  
**Prerequisite:** Access to an approved AI surface and approved, non-sensitive practice material. F101.2 requires a surface that can inspect the material and save a separate artifact.  
**Length:** Eight lessons, approximately 29 minutes, plus a 15–30 minute capstone

## Lesson format

Every lesson opens with a reference to the Analytics AI Enablement education series and the lesson topic. It closes by inviting learners to explore other videos and send feedback.

Each series bookend must take no more than 10 seconds of narration.

## Promise

Complete one safe, useful, independently verified task and learn the minimum shared vocabulary needed to continue in either 201 track.

## Placement exercise

A learner may place out by submitting one artifact and an evidence card showing that they can:

- Write a Goal / Data & tools / Context / Output / Manual verification task brief.
- Explain model, tools, context, memory, MCP, and the agentic loop in plain language.
- Select an approved model tier for a stated reason.
- Independently verify the artifact.
- Distinguish access, permission, correctness, and recovery.

## Outcomes

By the end, the learner can choose a contained task, write a clear task brief, produce and verify an artifact, explain core agentic vocabulary, choose a model deliberately, and select either 201 course without depending on the other.

## Capstone: one verified artifact

Use an approved AI surface to produce a useful artifact from approved source material. Preserve the source, independently verify the result, and submit the Foundation evidence card from the [learner workbook](../../shared/learner-workbook.md).

### Rubric

Score each criterion 0, 1, or 2. Passing is 8/10 with no zero on source integrity or verification.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task framing | Vague or missing | Some constraints stated | Goal, data and tools, context, output, and manual verification are clear |
| Source integrity | Source changed or unapproved | Integrity unclear | Approved source preserved |
| Artifact usefulness | No usable output | Partly usable | Concrete output fits the stated use |
| Verification | No independent check | One weak check | Coverage, exact evidence, and conclusion/test checked |
| Human ownership | Delegated consequential decision | Ownership implied | Human decision owner and residual uncertainty are explicit |
