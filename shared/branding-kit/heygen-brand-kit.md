# HeyGen Brand Kit

Approved configuration for the Aledade AI Learning Program.

## Colors

| HeyGen role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `#003DA5` | Primary brand blue, headings, and key graphics |
| Secondary | `#EAF2FF` | Light panels and supporting backgrounds |
| Tertiary | `#FF6900` | Signals, dividers, and restrained highlights |
| Accent | `#091F31` | Body copy and high-contrast dark elements |

Use white as the default canvas background.

## Typography

- Title text: Literata SemiBold
- Body text: IBM Plex Sans

## HeyGen settings

- Motion graphics: Elevated
- Primary logo: None

The motion style can be changed to a calmer editorial or minimal option later without changing the colors or typography.
