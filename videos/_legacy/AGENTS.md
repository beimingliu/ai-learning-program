# Legacy lesson videos

These lessons (F101.0 to F101.5, B201.7) were built before `shared/video-kit/` and are published. Keep them for re-renders and small fixes only.

- **Do not copy them as a template.** Their cues are hand-timed in seconds or they have no `src/` step. Start new lessons from `videos/foundations-101/F101.7-agentic-system` with the `/lesson-video` skill.
- To fix one, edit it in place. F101.5 uses its own `python3 build.py` for `src/` → `compositions/`. F101.4 and B201.7 are edited in `compositions/` directly. F101.0 to F101.3 hold only docs and snapshots (their videos were avatar A-roll with no HyperFrames project), so there is nothing to re-render. Do not port a legacy lesson to the kit unless the user asks.
- The final MP4s stay in `videos/` under their canonical lesson names.
