## Intent

Make new AI users see why MCP matters: without a connection Claude is sealed off from email, tickets, and threads; with MCP it picks a named tool, calls it, and reads the result. Then introduce the Gateway, the six services worth learning first, and Glean research as the time saver. Voice-only build narrated by the owner's cloned voice.

## Customizations

- Owner direction: follow the brand colors and fonts, but lean on creativity over discipline in the animation. This lesson departs from the card-and-terminal shell of F101.1 to F101.4.
- No screenshots of live tools (PHI). Every surface is a mocked component; all names, messages, tickets, and documents are invented and generic (Team A, Metric X, ANALYTICS-123).
- Recurring world: a Claude node, tool-call chips (`service · tool_name`) that travel to service tiles and return as result cards, a single MCP plug, and a Gateway hub.
- Dark ink scenes for the "connected world" beats and white scenes for explanation beats, both using only brand roles.
- Opening and Continue Learning close keep the series structure.
