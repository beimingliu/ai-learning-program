# F101.3 course frame

Use the A-1 presenter-left split as the default teaching world: the presenter occupies a dark-ink, organically cropped left stage, while the right canvas carries one concrete distinction at a time. Blue shows the harness and active context; pale blue groups supporting information; orange marks drift, human judgment, and actions.

The full-frame breakout scenes are evidence surfaces rather than slides. The three familiar replies read like transcript evidence, with a visible “Presenter heuristic” boundary. The compact-versus-new-session decision is a directional branch whose wrong path visibly transports the bad assumption into the summary.

Concept angle: make context feel like a deliberately assembled workbench that can fill up or go stale, not an invisible intelligence meter.

Focal hierarchy: one large teaching statement, one mechanism or decision graphic, and small edge metadata. Keep the left presenter stage free of teaching copy so Avatar V can be integrated without reflow.
